How it works

Three steps to found

Create your circle

Name it after tonight, the trip, or the group chat that never dies. You get a private invite code.

Share the code

Codes are single-circle and expire after a week — the only way in is being invited by someone already there.

Find each other

Turn on sharing when you head out. Everyone appears on the same map until you're together — then switch it off.

Privacy by design

Your location is nobody's business model

Together runs no ads, embeds no analytics, and never sells data. Your position is visible only to the circle you chose, encrypted in transit, and enforced row-by-row in the database — even a signed-in user can only ever read the circles they belong to.
