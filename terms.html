1. What Together is

Together lets small, invite-only circles of friends share their live location with each other and chat. Joining a circle takes an invite code and the circle owner's approval. A free circle holds up to three people and free accounts belong to one circle at a time; a Together Plus subscription (section 7) raises the capacity of circles its owner runs to eight and allows membership in up to five circles. Location sharing is always started by you, visible only to your circle, and can be stopped by you at any time.

Together is not an emergency or safety-critical service. Locations can be delayed, inaccurate, or unavailable — never rely on Together to locate someone in an emergency. Call your local emergency number instead.
